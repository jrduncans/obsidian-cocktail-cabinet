# Bottle classification source notes

Researched 2026-10-31 for the [knowledge bottle coverage review](knowledge-bottle-coverage-review.md). These are product facts from producers, brand owners, and their importers/distributors, followed by proposed cabinet classifications. The classifications are judgments for review, not producer-endorsed substitutions or approved starter additions. No private stock quantities or purchasing data are included.

The subsequent starter review added Contemporary Gin, Crème de Menthe under Herbal Liqueur, Vermouth Amaro, and Vanilla Syrup. Swedish Punsch and Sloe Gin remain outside the starter. Coconut Rum remains intentionally omitted, and the user rejects automatic shared-type grouping of Cut & Dry and Coco Atoll. The research proposals below predate those decisions; the [coverage review](knowledge-bottle-coverage-review.md) records the current outcomes.

## Unresolved primary-source checks

- **Gamle Ode Dill Aquavit:** the distributor verifies fresh dill, caraway, and juniper, but does not explicitly state the aging treatment on the reviewed page. [Bowler Wine](https://www.bowlerwine.com/wine-or-spirit/dill-aquavit/nv). **Unaged Aquavit → Aquavit** is a likely candidate, pending confirmation of the expression's age treatment. **Dill Aquavit → Aquavit** remains a valid custom role if the user wants to preserve dill-led recipe specificity. Do not infer no aging solely from a clear appearance or an omitted age statement.
- **Doctor Bird Pineapple Rum:** the named product supports preliminary **Pineapple Rum → Rum** placement rather than ordinary Aged Overproof Jamaican Rum. The producer's current indexed spirits pages did not expose a pineapple expression page, and guessed product URLs failed. Detailed production claims were therefore not treated as primary-source verified here. Preserve the named bottle and its recorded strength; do not imply equal strength with Stiggins' Fancy.
- **Townshend's Bluebird Alpine Liqueur:** a live producer/importer product description could not be verified. Preserve its existing **Alpine Liqueur** identity as a custom type under **Herbal Liqueur** pending clarification. The word alpine alone is insufficient to merge its recipe role with Braulio's **Alpine Amaro**. This is a review flag, not evidence that the old vault classification was necessarily wrong.

## Proposed review priorities

The strongest structural gap in this subset is **Contemporary Gin**: several non-London-Dry gin profiles need an honest type. Other established recipe identities worth considering despite one bottle each are **Sloe Gin**, **Swedish Punsch**, and **Crème de Menthe** (the latter is outside this memo's product research). **French Brandy** is a separate accuracy gap because St-Rémy cannot faithfully be assigned Cognac. Alpine herbal/amaro boundaries and highly flavored gin/amaro variants need deliberate definitions before treating shared type membership as matching behavior.

No catalog or vault changes were made as part of this research.
